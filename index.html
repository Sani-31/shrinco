About us

Built to defined dimensions, built to last.

Shrinco Cable Support System Private Limited manufactures cable tray systems, fittings, supports and earthing material for industrial, commercial and infrastructure projects. Products are made to defined dimensions and finishes so installations are neat, safe and built to last. We supply EPC contractors, electrical contractors and project teams.

Vision

To be a trusted name in cable management and industrial support solutions, known for engineering excellence and long-term customer relationships.

Mission

To manufacture reliable, application-oriented products with a strong focus on quality, customer requirements and continuous improvement.

Core Values

Quality · Precision · Commitment · Integrity · Improvement · Partnership
